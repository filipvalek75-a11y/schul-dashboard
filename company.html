Monthly business review

Current e-commerce research watch · September 2026

Ideas to investigate for Omnidruck before implementation. These are not assumptions that the company currently uses Shopify or Google Merchant Center.

AI / agentic product discovery
Shopping is increasingly moving into AI conversations. Priority check: make product titles, descriptions, attributes, pricing and availability structured and machine-readable so products can be understood by AI shopping surfaces.
AI-channel commerce
Shopify's 2026 updates emphasize Catalog, Agentic Storefronts and UCP, while Google is expanding UCP-based shopping. If Omnidruck's stack can support equivalent structured feeds, evaluate AI-shopping visibility as a future acquisition channel.
Conversion experimentation
Modern commerce platforms are adding native testing and AI-assisted optimization. Build a simple test backlog for product-page copy, calls-to-action, enquiry flows and checkout friction instead of changing pages without measurement.
